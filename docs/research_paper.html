Content Opportunity Scoring: Predictive Content Decay & Machine Learning Architecture

Track: Lane 1 — Content Opportunity Scoring | Framework: FlyRank Search Intelligence Capstone

Abstract

Summary: Identifying declining organic web content prior to severe traffic drops is vital for targeted SEO updates. This paper details a machine learning approach to content opportunity scoring using Google Search Console data. By replacing static heuristic rule sets with a Supervised Random Forest Classifier trained on leakage-free performance metrics and split via GroupShuffleSplit across domain entities, our ML model achieves superior predictive performance over baseline strategies.

1. Data Extraction & Feature Engineering

Data was queried from Search Console warehouse records using DuckDB. To ensure the model remains robust against target leakage, 5 strictly historical feature vectors were synthesized over 30-day aggregation windows:

  • total_impressions: Sum of Google Search Console impressions.
  • total_clicks: Sum of organic traffic clicks generated.
  • avg_ctr: Weighted Click-Through Rate ($clicks / impressions$).
  • avg_position: Mean organic search ranking position.
  • active_days_ratio: Consistency ratio of days active within the observation window.

Cross-Validation Integrity

To prevent data contamination between training and evaluation datasets, records were partitioned using GroupShuffleSplit conditioned on client_hash_id. This guarantees that all performance records belonging to a specific client domain exist exclusively within either the training or testing set.

3. Business Impact & Deployment

The comparative evaluation confirms that static rule sets yield high false positive rates, causing content operations teams to waste resources refreshing stable pages. The supervised machine learning model provides actionable content opportunity scores, enabling content strategy teams to prioritize URL re-optimization based on real predictive decay signals.
